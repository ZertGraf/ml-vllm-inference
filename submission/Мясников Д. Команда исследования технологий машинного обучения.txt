Repository: https://github.com/ZertGraf/ml-vllm-inference

Candidate: Dmitry Myasnikov (Myasnikov D.), ML technology research team - Data Engineer intern test task.
Solution: vLLM 0.32.0 (CPU only) with Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct on Minikube; rootless images with hash-pinned dependencies, k8s manifests with kustomize (kubectl apply -k), Secret/ConfigMap/PVC configuration.
Privacy: logging filter + request-fact middleware keep prompts and generations out of stdout/stderr (verified with canary strings, including a negative control); Bearer auth guard also closes the routes vLLM's --api-key leaves open.
Bonuses: kustomize overlays, model download in a separate rootless initContainer image, load test (TTFT/latency/throughput) with the tuning analysis in README.md.
